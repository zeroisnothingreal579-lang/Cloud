    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is this a real phone or an emulator?

    A real, dedicated Android instance running on server-grade hardware with ARM virtualization — not a shared emulator. It has its own device ID, IMEI-range identifier, storage and network egress.

    Do my phones keep running when I close the browser?

    Yes. Streaming disconnects, but the instance keeps running 24/7 — games farm, scripts run, builds install. Reconnect anytime and pick up exactly where you left off.

    Can I install any APK or use the Play Store?

    Yes. Upload APKs directly, use the built-in store, or push builds over ADB. Google Mobile Services images are available on Pro and Ultra tiers.

    How many phones can I run at once?

    Up to 50 per account on self-serve, thousands via enterprise contracts. Multi-control lets you mirror touches to the whole fleet at once.

    Is it allowed for games and automation?

    Yes for your own accounts and testing. You must still respect each app's terms of service — we provide the hardware, you own the behavior.
